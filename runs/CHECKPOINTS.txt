    train_STDC2-Seg-Baseline    0.7600   2026-07-30  (pre-cutoff, not in runs/cityscapes_commands.txt)
    train_STDC2-Seg-Baseline2   0.7512   2026-08-11  (no --bnd_weight, so default 1.0)
    train_STDC2-Seg-I0          0.7635   2026-08-09  (--bnd_weight 1.0, explicit)

USE I0 as the single baseline in the paper:
  - it is inside your cutoff,
  - its command is recorded verbatim in runs/cityscapes_commands.txt (Baseline's is not),
  - it is the explicit lambda=1 control, so I0 -> I1 is a true one-variable change.

BUT report Baseline2 as a seed replicate rather than dropping it, because:
  spread across identical configs = 0.7512 .. 0.7635 = 0.0123 mIoU
  your headline effect  I0 -> HI1 = 0.7635 -> 0.7725 = 0.0090 mIoU
  => on FULL mIoU the headline gain is SMALLER than seed noise.
  This is fine for your argument only because full mIoU is your guard metric,
  not your primary one. The boundary/thin-class deltas are 2-4x larger
  (bnd r=1 -0.0198, thin r=1 -0.0319 for I1 vs HI1). Say this explicitly in
  the paper before a reviewer says it for you.
